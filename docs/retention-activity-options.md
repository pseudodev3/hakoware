# Retention activity candidates — 2026-10-06

Neither activity is implemented or selected. The user rejected vague prompts, Hunter Runs/maps, and the weekly wall. The next feature needs to be enjoyable without an XP/Aura reward and useful with one active friend.

## Private card duel — recommended to explore first

A short hidden-hand game inside Arena between accepted friends. Both receive the same free match deck. On your turn, draw a card, choose which of your two cards to play, and keep the other hidden. A played card changes what you know or what the other player can do:

- Watcher peeks at their held card.
- Ghost protects you until your next turn.
- Orbit swaps hidden hands.
- Clean Slate replaces your held card.
- Signal Flare lets you guess their held card; a correct guess ends the round.
- Oath has the highest held value, but discarding it loses the round.

When the deck runs out, the higher remaining card wins. These are proposed match-only versions using the existing artwork, separate from the purchased contract tools and inventory. Exact deck counts/values need playtesting; this is not a production rules commitment.

The interesting moment: you watch their hand and see Oath. They see that you played Watcher. Before you can guess, they must decide whether to protect themselves, swap hands or replace the exposed card. Your next choice depends on their real move, rather than a daily instruction.

First use: start a free match with one accepted friend and play the first card. Finish in a few minutes together or let the other person take their turn later. The reason to return is their move, a reveal or a rematch. Results belong in the existing friend space, with a clear turn destination.

No Aura wagers, paid power, inventory consumption, contract debt, required streaks or reward claim step. A single-account practice opponent could teach the rules, but should not pretend to be a real participant or stand in for social retention. The main risk is asynchronous matches dragging; test the actual round with a pair of friends before adding progression. The useful signal is whether both voluntarily want a rematch without rewards.

## Reusable personal voice reactions — social companion

Save a short recording of your own joke/catchphrase, choose an existing card image as its button, then send it with one tap inside a friend conversation. A clip remains useful during ordinary chat, after the first creation session is over. Playback is deliberate; no autoplay or new public feed.

First use: record a two-to-eight-second reaction, send it, and hear the friend's reply. The reason to return is the real exchange; saved clips make Hakoware a more personal place to talk. Received clips are reusable only within that duo when the creator explicitly allows it.

No daily clip assignment, rewards for production, or requirement to fill a board. This could give retained collectible art a conversational use once designed, without changing owned cards silently in the retirement pass. It depends on users liking audio humor, and would need real iPhone recording/playback testing. It strengthens chat but is a weaker standalone reason to return than a game people want to replay.

## Selection principle

Explore the duel first if the goal is a concrete activity people can choose to play. Explore voice reactions if the goal is making existing friendship conversations distinctive. Neither guarantees retention; use actual play/reply/rematch behavior to decide whether to expand it.
