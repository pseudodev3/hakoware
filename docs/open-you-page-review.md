# A more open You page

The profile summary, signature-card display, and collection link now sit directly on the page. The bank loses its outer frame and icon box, while the gold credit panel and current credit tier retain their emphasis. Other credit tiers use fine separators. The featured Duo, actionable grudge cards, and form controls keep their containers.

Spacing, shared left edges, hover cues, and smaller ledger dates give the open sections clear hierarchy. This change updates three stylesheets; account, card, and bank behavior is unchanged.

The production build and whitespace checks pass. Chromium checks cover populated and empty profiles at 320, 390, 768, and 1280 pixels in both themes, plus 40 focused section/dialog views. No horizontal overflow or unexpected browser errors occurred. The signature editor, focus return, repayment confirmation, borrowing checklist, sounds switch, privacy preference, and collection/Circle navigation remain usable. Disposable local accounts and their records were removed after review.
